Settings holds the options that apply to all of Phoenix, in six windows.

- **{{label:workspace.aiProviders}}**: the AI accounts Phoenix uses, model catalog checks and the optional {{label:routing.providerSection}}.
- **{{label:settings.agentcliCard}}**: how Agent Chat behaves.
- **{{label:workspace.homeAssistant}}**: {{label:settings.voiceCard}}, {{label:settings.aiTaskCard}} and {{label:settings.assistCard}} connect Phoenix to Home Assistant's own AI features.
- **{{label:workspace.safety}}**: the {{label:settings.killSwitchCard}}, MESA enforcement and {{label:settings.dataCard}}.
- **{{label:workspace.logsNotifications}}**: what is recorded, and when Home Assistant notifies you.
- **{{label:workspace.general}}**: language, theme, optional features and version information.

**{{label:workspace.findSetting}}** on the toolbar finds a setting and opens the window it is in.
