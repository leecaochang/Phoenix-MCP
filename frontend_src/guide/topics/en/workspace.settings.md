Settings holds the options that apply to all of Phoenix.

- **{{label:settings.providersCard}}** and **{{label:settings.agentcliCard}}**: the AI accounts Phoenix uses, and how Agent Chat behaves.
- **{{label:settings.voiceCard}}**, **{{label:settings.aiTaskCard}}** and **{{label:settings.assistCard}}** connect Phoenix to Home Assistant's own AI features.
- **{{label:settings.killSwitchCard}}** stops Phoenix serving every agent request at once.
- **{{label:settings.loggingCard}}** and **{{label:settings.notificationsCard}}** decide what is recorded and when Home Assistant notifies you.
