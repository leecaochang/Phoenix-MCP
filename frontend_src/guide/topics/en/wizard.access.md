{ "id": "wizard.access", "title": "Granting Access to One Light" }
---
A new key can reach nothing until you grant it something. Grant one light you can see, so the test shows the whole path working.

Expand the light group and choose **W** on a light for read and write access. A grant on a device covers its entities; a grant on the whole domain covers every light, including ones added later.

If this Home Assistant has no lights, **{{label:wizard.showAllDevices}}** lists every domain. You can grant more later in the permissions tree.
