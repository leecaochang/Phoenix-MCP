---
A new key can reach nothing until you grant it something. Grant one light you can see, so the test shows everything working.

Expand the light group and choose **W** on a light, for read and write access. A grant on a device covers its entities. A grant on the whole domain covers every light, including ones added later.

If this Home Assistant has no lights, **{{label:wizard.showAllDevices}}** lists every domain. You can grant more later in the {{label:accessKeys.permissionsTree}}.
