---
A persona is a starting set of capabilities: which kinds of tools the agent may use, and which of them wait for your approval first.

The preselected persona is the cautious choice for a first test. Pick another if you already know the agent's job; you can change single capabilities later in [Access Keys](guide:workspace.accessKeys).

A persona never decides which devices the agent can reach. That comes from the access you grant two steps from now.
